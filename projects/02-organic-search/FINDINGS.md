# What changed in this example?

Across the fictional three-page-group dataset, impressions rise from 39,000 to 44,500 (+14.10%), clicks increase from 1,650 to 1,965 (+19.09%), and overall CTR rises from approximately 4.23% to 4.42%.

The overall CTR is calculated as **total clicks / total impressions**, not the unweighted average of the three page-group percentages. March's illustrative editorial group has 378 clicks from 10,800 impressions (3.50%), versus February's 270 from 9,000 (3.00%).

### Before suggesting an SEO action
Check search queries, branded vs non-branded traffic, search appearance, page positions, device and country. Verify that measurement dates and site changes align. Two monthly snapshots cannot isolate causes.

### Data scope
Synthetic practice data. This is a reconstructed analytical exercise, not claimed historical Search Console performance.
