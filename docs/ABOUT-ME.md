# About me

I'm **Liban Yusuf**, based in Oslo, with a bachelor's degree in Marketing and Sales Management. My practical background includes digital analytics and business reporting. I enjoy figuring out what a business actually needs to know before choosing a metric or a tool.

## How I work
I begin with the question and the definitions, explore the available source data, use smaller checks to catch mistakes, and then explain the finding in plain language. When information is missing, I would rather show the limitation than offer false precision.

## Two complementary areas
- **Marketing Analytics | February–31 March 2026:** website measurement, audience questions, SEO and campaign KPIs. This public portfolio uses freshly prepared synthetic case materials to present that chapter.
- **Data Analytics | April–October 2026:** SQL, Python, customer and revenue analysis, validation and business intelligence learning. The public SQL exercises are linked from the [learning path](../LEARNING-PATH.md).

## Tools in context
GA4, GTM and Search Console support distinct marketing measurement questions; Excel helps define and review reporting; SQL and Python help inspect, transform and check data; Power BI, DAX and Microsoft Fabric are areas being developed, not finished implementations represented by this repo.

I'm interested in entry-level analyst work where I can contribute with business understanding, careful analysis and continued technical development.
